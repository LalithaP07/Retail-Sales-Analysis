# Retail-Sales-Analysis
This project's goal is to: 
Analyze the data to discover insights that can help the firm perform better. 
Learn more about things like:- Product categories with the highest profit margins Geographic areas with the highest sales and profits
Make suggestions for the business on how to boost sales, revenue, and profitability. 

Project research objectives

I am mainly focusing on understanding the factors that contribute to high sales in the retail stores. 
1. Which product categories in the store have the biggest profit margins? 
2. Do the east region's sales significantly differ from those in the other areas?
3. How do sales fluctuate by product category throughout the course of the year?
4. what is the rate of returned products for orders with same-day shipping compared to other shipping options?
5. How do weekday sales and profits compare to weekend sales by product category?
